## 2. What the marketplace is

**SETTLED**
- A **separate third space**. Everything in it costs money. The File Board and Experience Board stay free. **Never mix them.**
- Members can list **items, videos, books, classes**, and similar, for money.
- AOEhub takes a **percentage of each sale**.
- AOEhub also sells its own **hub merch and content merch** (section 12).

**OPEN — the percentage**
- A. One flat percentage on top of payment-processing fees.
- B. A lower percentage for sellers with a track record.
- C. A flat fee per sale.
- D. A small launch period with no cut, then a flat percentage.
- Also decide who pays the processor's fee: the seller (out of their share) or AOEhub (out of its cut).

## 7. Previews, returns, and scams

**SETTLED**
- **Every listing needs a sample or preview.** For physical items the photos/video do this job. For digital goods it means real sample content — a few pages, a short clip, an excerpt.
- **Returns apply to physical items only.** Digital goods cannot be returned, because of the copy-then-ask-for-money-back risk.
- **A seller scam is a report, not a refund.** It goes to the Watchers and Deacons to review.
- **No guarantee against scams.** AOEhub does not guarantee or take responsibility for scams between members; this is stated in the Terms of Service. **REMINDER: a real lawyer writes that clause — Clara asked to be reminded.**

**OPEN**
- **7a. If a scam report is upheld, does the buyer get any money back?**
  - A. Never: report and penalty on the seller only.
  - B. Possibly: refund from a held payout if reviewers uphold the report within the hold window.
  - C. Case by case at the Creator's discretion.
  - D. Refunds only for a narrow list (never delivered, or nothing like the listing).
- **7b. A digital file that is broken or not what was listed.**
  - A. No refund, ever.
  - B. A narrow exception decided by a person (Watcher, Deacon, or Creator), who can check the file opens and matches the listing.
  - C. Automatic refund (not recommended: invites copy-then-refund).
- **7c. Physical returns.** Length of the window (Clara mentioned "7 days or something"), who pays return shipping, required condition, and whether the seller or AOEhub sets the policy.
- **7d. Payout hold.**
  - A. Pay the seller immediately.
  - B. Hold a short window (e.g., a day or two) after a sale or download.
  - C. Hold until the buyer confirms or a set number of days pass.
  - D. Longer holds for new sellers, shorter for trusted ones.
- **7e. Chargebacks.** A buyer's bank can reverse a payment no matter what the Terms say. Decide who absorbs the cost: the seller (usual), AOEhub, or shared.

## 8. Parents selling a child's work

**SETTLED**
- A parent may sell their child's **art, design, code, or any eye-catching project**.
- The **parent is the seller** (the account, the payout). The child never needs their own account — this also fits the 13+ account rule.
- The listing shows **the child's name or the child's pen name, plus age.** The family chooses: *"Child's name is ___"* or *"Child's pen name is ___."* A real name is never required.
- A child's **pen name can be used on everything that child makes**, if they want. (Read as: every listing by that child — not hub-wide, since under-13s cannot hold accounts. *Correct this if you meant more.*)

**OPEN**
- **Age display.** A. Exact age. B. An age range (e.g., 7–9). C. A broad label (child/teen).
- **Where pen names live.** A. A reusable child profile saved under the parent's account. B. Typed fresh on each listing.
- **Photos of the child.** A. None. B. Only of the work. C. Allowed with the parent's choice.
- **13–17-year-old account holders.** Payment processors generally require the person getting paid to be an adult. A. They cannot sell; a parent sells for them. B. They sell through a parent-linked account.
- **Minors buying.** A. Buyers must be 18+. B. Minors can buy only with a parent's payment method.
- **Legal read** on a child's name and age appearing publicly, even with a pen-name option, and whether a grown child can later ask for their name or listings to be removed.

**SETTLED**
- Watchers and Deacons receive **new rules and new powers** at the update. Scam reports on sellers go to them to review.
- Everything they do is recorded as it is today, and every action needs a reason from a fixed list (the lists are extended with marketplace reasons).

**OPEN**
- **9a. Which powers.** Possible: view a reported listing and its comments (and any reported private conversation, if 13a keeps private messaging); add a notice; warn; remove a listing; suspend someone's selling; hold a payout. Options: A. Watchers get all, Deacons fewer (today Deacons handle posts only and listings would be a third kind). B. Both get the same. C. Start small and add.
- **9b. Existing holders.** A. They receive the new powers automatically, with notice and re-acceptance of the updated reviewer rules. B. Reviewing the marketplace is a separate role people opt into.
- **9c. Can staff sell? — SETTLED (Clara, 2026-10-07, refined 2026-10-08).** A Watcher or Deacon is an ordinary member account with an extra job, reached through a private link on their profile. In their **normal member place** they can post, upload, comment and **upload items to the marketplace** like any member. **Inside the Watcher/Deacon panel they cannot post, upload files or list marketplace items**: the panel never contains a sell action, and the server refuses it on any panel request. The **Creator role** does not post or sell; the Creator uses an ordinary account for that. Only the Creator and the Watchers and Deacons themselves know who holds these roles, so a staff member's listings look like anyone's. AOEhub's own merch is sold by an official store account, not a personal one. *Rule for their own listings (decided by Clara, 2026-10-08, same as posts):* a Watcher or Deacon **can see** a report about their own listing but **cannot dismiss, delete, warn on or otherwise act on it**; any other Watcher (or the Creator) can. Because a listing is more like a file than a post, it is expected to follow the Watcher/Creator side, not the Deacon side (Deacons handle posts and comments only); confirm this when moderation of listings is designed. The Terms say staff are members like any other when selling.
- **9d. Reading private messages** (only applies if 13a keeps any private messaging). Only inside a reported conversation, disclosed in the Terms.

## 10. Verified-only sellers — OPEN. Clara wants this discussed LAST.

**Why it is hard:** today "Verified" means the Creator agrees with the person's ideas. It is a philosophy badge, granted by hand — not a financial-trust check, and it does not scale. The goal is to keep scams down at scale without choking growth.

**Options**
- A. **Verified required to sell.** Strict; reviewed by hand; a bottleneck as the hub grows.
- B. **Only the payment processor's identity check.** Scales automatically; no extra gate.
- C. **Both** Verified and the processor's check. Strictest.
- D. **Processor check plus rules:** account age, no active red warnings, some activity on the hub. Automatic and scalable.
- E. **Tiers.** New sellers start limited (few listings, lower prices, longer payout hold) and unlock more with a track record; Verified unlocks the top tier.
- F. **Start strict (A or C) while small, loosen to D or E later.**

**Connects to:** the warnings system (a red warning for scamming could suspend selling), 7a, and 7d.

## 11. Payments

**SETTLED**
- The audience is **global**.
- **Stripe, using Stripe Connect,** is the main payment system. It handles many sellers plus a platform cut, and verifies sellers' identity before they are paid.
- Buyers can pay from about 135 countries and currencies. The business holding the account must be in one of the roughly 40 countries Stripe supports (**not** mainland China).
- Clara wants **WeChat Pay and Alipay**. Stripe supports both so that Chinese buyers can pay a business based elsewhere.
- **PayPal** is a possible extra: it is separate from Stripe, so it would be a second payment button.
- **AOEhub is not registered as a business yet** and has not decided where or how.

**OPEN**
- **11a. Where and how to register AOEhub.** A. Where Clara personally lives (usually simplest: local bank account, taxes already filed there). B. A different Stripe-supported country (more friction: possibly a local director or agent). C. Wait until closer to the marketplace launch. *Needs an accountant/lawyer.* This decides everything below.
- **11b. WeChat Pay/Alipay inside Stripe Connect specifically.** An older source suggested WeChat Pay under Connect may be limited or invite-only. *Confirm directly with Stripe before relying on it.*
- **11c. PayPal.** Add it, or leave it out.
- **11d. Other regional payment methods.** Choose from Stripe's list once the registration country is known.
- **11e. Currency.** A. One currency (e.g., USD) for everything. B. Sellers price in their own currency and Stripe converts. C. Show the buyer an estimate in their local currency.
- **11f. Seller payouts.** Schedule, minimum payout, and which countries sellers can be paid in (this varies by country).
- **11g. Where payment code runs.** Payments need server-side code (secret keys never go in the browser; Stripe sends confirmations to a server address). A. Supabase Edge Functions (works wherever the site is hosted). B. Hosting that can run server code.

**Reference — steps to wire up payments:** (1) decide where AOEhub is based; (2) create the Stripe account and complete business verification, enabling Connect; (3) turn on payment methods in the Stripe dashboard; (4) add Stripe to the hub's code, including seller onboarding; (5) set the platform's cut; (6) test fully in sandbox mode; (7) switch to live mode, with tax/compliance handled first.

## 13. Trust and safety rules for the marketplace — all OPEN

- **13a. Talking with the seller — OPEN (Clara is leaning toward public comments; not decided).** Private messaging would be the hub's *first* member-to-member private messaging (today the only private messages are reviewer notices), and it is also the commonest scam route ("pay me directly instead"). Options: **A. Public comments only** on the item page, so everyone sees every question and answer; no private messages at all. Pros: scams and "pay me directly" offers are visible to everyone and to reviewers, one answer helps every buyer, far less to build and moderate, no private-message privacy issues. Cons: buyers cannot share order details privately, and some people will not ask in public. **B. Private messages**, with limits: only about a specific listing; no links or payment/contact details (auto-warn or block); messages kept as evidence; a report button. **C. Public comments for everything, plus a private thread opened only between a buyer and seller after a purchase** (for delivery and order problems), with the same limits. **D. Public comments, plus a "report privately to Watchers/Deacons" button instead of buyer-to-seller private chat.** Whichever is chosen, a rule is needed that order details, addresses and payment details never go in public comments.
- **13b. Off-platform payment.** Forbidden. Consequence options: a red warning, or a warning first.
- **13c. Review integrity.** Only real purchasers may review; sellers cannot review themselves; fake reviews are removed; sellers may reply.
- **13d. What may be sold.** A. Educational only (curriculum, books, classes, children's art/crafts, hub merch). B. Anything legal. Likely banned either way: weapons, adult content, pirated or counterfeit work, health claims. Physical children's products (toys, kits) have safety rules that differ by country.
- **13e. Physical goods.** Seller ships; the free-delivery flag; international shipping and customs; tracking; lost or damaged parcels; keeping sellers' addresses private. (Avoided entirely by release option 1A.)
- **13f. The warnings ladder.** A. One record per person across the hub and marketplace. B. A separate marketplace record. Also: does a 3-red restriction bar selling, and does 5 red still go to the Creator?
- **13g. Reasons per section.** "Paywalled or costs money" is a yellow warning on the free boards; it must not apply in the marketplace.

## 14. Keeping the free boards free — 14a and 14b settled for now, the rest OPEN

Reselling free files — SETTLED for now (Clara, 2026-10-07): allowed.** Users may free-download a File Board item, remix it, and choose to upload the result as a paid item; users may also take back their **own** uploaded items and list them as paid in the marketplace. - **14d. Points.** A. Marketplace activity earns no points. B. A separate marketplace score.
