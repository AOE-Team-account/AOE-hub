## 1. Who is responsible
AOE Team runs AOEhub. Questions about your data: info@aoe.name.

## 2. What we hold
- **Your account:** email address, password (stored scrambled by the sign-in service), name, optional display name, a yes to "I am 13 or older" (not a birth date), and which version of the Terms you accepted and when.
- **Your answers** to the optional questions when you first sign in, and the region and language you chose.
- **What you post:** experience posts, comments, files and their labels, previews, your profile picture and look, reports and appeals you send, notifications, who you follow, and points.
- **Views and downloads** of posts and files, once per person per day, for counts and points.
- **Safety records:** warnings (with a copy of the content at the time), restrictions, who acted and when, kept as evidence.
- **Your own AI key,** if you add one: stored encrypted and never shown again.
- **Technical data:** your IP address appears in the logs of our hosting and database companies and is used to limit abuse.

## 3. Why
To run your account, show your posts, keep the hub safe, answer questions with the hub AI, translate content, and meet legal duties.

## 4. Who else receives data
- **Supabase** (database and sign-in) holds all account data.
- **Cloudflare R2** stores uploaded files, pictures and hosted games.
- **Internet Archive** stores large files. **Files there are public and permanent and cannot be removed by us.**
- **Backblaze B2** holds nightly backups of the database.
- **Google** (Gemini) answers questions and translates text, and makes the search data for the AI. **Groq** does the same when Google is full. You cannot tell which one answered. **On the free plans these companies may use what is sent and people may read it.** Do not put private information into the hub AI. If you add your own AI key, that company receives your questions instead.
- **Names of languages** you ask for, and text to be translated, are also sent to Google or Groq.
- **VirusTotal** receives files and videos to check them for malware.
- **Brevo** sends our emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset).
- **Namecheap** hosts the website and domain.

## 5. How long we keep things
| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Deleted posts, files and comments | 30 days in the trash, then erased |
| A deleted account (by you or by the Creator) | Switched off at once, erased after 30 days; until then it can be restored |
| Warnings, restrictions, reports and safety records about that account | Erased with the account on day 30 |
| Backups | Daily for 30 days, then weekly for up to a year, so older copies can still hold data you deleted |
| The AI's copy of your content | Removed when you delete the content |
| Files on the Internet Archive | Public and permanent |
| Translations of your text | Removed with the text |

## 6. Your rights
You can see and correct your data, delete your account, and download a copy in Settings ("Download my data"). You can also write to us. [[Lawyer: rights under the laws that apply to your members.]]

## 7. Children
You must be 13 or older. A younger child's account should be created and managed by a parent or guardian. Please do not use a photo of a child as a profile picture.

## 8. Cookies and similar storage
We use cookies for your sign-in and for your chosen language (`aoehub.lang`), and your browser's storage for theme, text size and a one-time welcome card. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. [[Lawyer: whether a consent banner is needed.]]

## 9. Changes
The version and date are shown on each page. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you.
