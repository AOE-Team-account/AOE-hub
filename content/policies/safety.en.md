## Our promise
We work hard to keep AOEhub safe, but we cannot guarantee that nothing bad will ever happen.

## If someone is in danger
Contact your local emergency services first. The hub's reviewers are people, not an emergency service. To tell us about a serious safety concern, use the Report button and tick "This is urgent". Urgent reports go to the top of every reviewer's list and the Creator is told at once.

## Child safety
Content that endangers or sexualises children is never allowed. The account can be suspended at once while the Creator decides. [[Lawyer: duties to report to the authorities and how such material must be handled.]]

## Staying safe with other members
Never send money to someone you only know from the hub. Be careful with links and files. Do not share private details about your family or children. Report anything that worries you.

## Files, games and links
Every uploaded file is checked for viruses before anyone can download it. Hosted games run in a protected box and cannot see your account. Links to other websites are not checked by us.

## The AI assistant and translation
They can be wrong. Do not rely on them for safety, medical or legal matters, and do not put private information into the hub AI.

## Reporting
Use the "⋯" button on a post, file or comment, or "Report this picture" on a profile. A reviewer looks and decides. Who reported something is not shown to the person reported. Copyright owners can send a formal notice on the Copyright page.

## How reviewers work
A small team of reviewers (Watchers, Deacons and the Creator) reads reports. Each action needs a reason from a fixed list, nobody reviews their own content, and everything is logged. Their roles are never shown publicly. You are told privately, never publicly, and you can appeal a decision.

## Records
Safety records about an account are kept as evidence while it exists and are erased 30 days after the account is deleted.
