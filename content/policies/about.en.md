Education isn't something you copy. It's something you walk.

AOEhub (Alpha Omega Education) is a free home for the philosophy, the honest experience and the curriculum behind education, shared by families who started the walk before you, for families just starting now. It is for homeschool, unschool and school families alike.

## What is free
The Experience Board and the File Board are free to read and, with an account, free to use. A marketplace is planned for later; we cannot promise what will happen.

## How it is run
A small review team keeps the hub safe. Their roles are kept private. [[How much to say about who is behind the hub.]]
