## The spirit
AOEhub is families walking their own path, sharing real experience, philosophy and free curriculum. You are free to disagree with an idea. Never target a person.

## How we treat each other
- Be respectful and honest.
- Give credit for other people's work.
- Help newcomers.
- Nobody has to follow any one method.

## Philosophy and faith
Discussion is welcome and should stay civil. Traditional faith, prayer and scripture are welcome. See the Content Policy for what is not allowed.

## Questions and answers
Ask freely. Answers from members with an **A** (Answerer) badge are pinned to the top. Members with a **V** (Verified) badge are people the hub's Creator agrees with, and their comments are highlighted. These are the only roles anyone can see.

## What the review team does
A small team reads reports and can add a notice, warn, restrict or suspend. You are told privately, with the reason. Warnings have levels (green, yellow, red). Repeated red warnings lead to a one-week restriction: you can read and download, but not post, upload or comment. Suspension is only for serious cases. You can appeal.

## Reporting
Use "⋯" on a post, file or comment. If it is urgent, tick "This is urgent".

## Asking about a decision
Open "My record and appeals" from your profile.
