AOEhub respects the rights of creators. Members must only post what they have the right to share.

## If you own the copyright to something posted here
You do not need an account. Use the [takedown form](/takedown). Please give:
- your full name and email address, and your postal address if you can;
- the work that was copied and where the original is;
- the address (link) of the post or file here;
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not allowed, and that your notice is accurate;
- your signature (your full name).

A person will look at it. Sending a false notice can have legal consequences.
