AOEhub exists to help parents cultivate a protected, wise and grounded learning environment for the next generation. Please read this with the Community Policy and the Safety Policy.

## What is not allowed
**Removed and the account banned**
- Child abuse, cruelty, neglect or emotional terror.
- Graphic, sexual or adult content.
- Hate speech, violence or dehumanisation based on race, origin or sex.
- Cultic recruitment, high-control groups and psychological manipulation.
- Religious extremism, calls to violence and spiritual abuse.
- Scams: educational scams, pyramid or high-pressure multi-level marketing, fake "miracle" cures.
- Selling or promoting hazardous health supplements or unverified medical practices.

**Removed, and you are warned (repeated warnings lead to restrictions)**
- Teaching or promoting LGBTQ+ ideas, gender theories or sexual-orientation ideas as right and to be welcomed, especially to children.
- Undermining parental authority, or encouraging children to keep secrets from their parents.
- Lesser scams.

**A notice is added, nothing is removed**
- "Twaddle": shallow busywork, vulgar content, mindless screen reliance.
- High-stress cramming, public shaming and high-pressure learning tactics.

## What stays
- A person saying these ideas are wrong, or telling parents how to avoid them.
- A parent asking how to answer a child's question about them.
- A faith statement.
- Traditional faith, prayer, scripture study, creationism and religious values.
- Traditional parental authority and loving discipline, in general terms. Anything that advocates cruelty, injury or abuse is not allowed.
- High-quality, classical learning materials and rich literature.
- Discussion of the soul, spiritual growth and moral character.
- Open inquiry into natural mysteries and unexplained phenomena, with wonder and critical thought rather than fear.

## Files, games and links
- Only upload what you have the right to share. Say whether it is your own work, a remix or not your work.
- Every uploaded file is checked for viruses before anyone can download it, and anything unsafe is deleted.
- A hosted game or app is a .zip of up to 50 MB with an index.html. Bigger ones link to an approved site, labelled "Hosted outside AOEhub".

## Profile pictures
Profile pictures follow the same rule: no graphic, sexual or adult content. You can report a picture; a reviewer looks first.

## What happens when something breaks a rule
See the Community Policy. You are always told privately, with the reason, and you can appeal.
