Rules for reviewers

Draft rules. The Creator edits these. Every Watcher and Deacon follows them, every time.

1. Every action needs a reason. Delete, warn, dismiss, and add a notice each ask you to pick one from a list. No reason, no action.

2. Act only on what breaks the written rules. Never because you disagree with someone or dislike their style.

3. The reason sets the warning level, not you. Green: off-topic. Yellow: ads, wrong ideas, paywalled, copyright. Red: scams, harmful ideology or content, inappropriate content.

4. Counts add up on their own. 10 green = 1 yellow. 5 yellow = 1 red. 3 red = 1 week restricted (no posting ideas, uploading files, or commenting). 5 red = you can report the account to the Creator, who decides.

5. Only the Creator deletes accounts, undoes warnings, or changes a notice the Creator added.

6. Don’t act on your own posts, comments or files, or on people you know personally. You can see a report about your own item, but you can’t dismiss, delete or warn on it — another Watcher or Deacon (or the Creator) will.

7. Everything you do is recorded with your name and the time, and kept as evidence. Misuse can cost you your role.

8. Deleting is never final. The Creator can restore anything.
