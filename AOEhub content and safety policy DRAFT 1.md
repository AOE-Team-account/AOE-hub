# Content Policy

AOEhub (Alpha Omega Education) is a free home for the philosophy, the honest experience, and the curriculum behind education. It stays useful and trustworthy because of what members share — and because of what we don't allow. This policy explains what belongs here and what doesn't.

These rules are about **what is posted and how people behave — never about who someone is.** Being a homeschooler, an unschooler, a school family, or any kind of person is never a violation. Disagreeing about how children should be educated is welcome; harming or scamming people is not.

## What we ask of everything you post

1. **Be honest about where it came from.** Mark every upload as Original, Remix, or Not my work. If you remixed someone's work, the link back to the original creator is added automatically — please don't remove it.
2. **Keep it free.** AOEhub is free forever. Don't post anything that costs money, sits behind a paywall, or pushes people toward buying something.
3. **Keep it about education.** Curriculum, books, songs, printables, games, apps, stories, and the ideas behind them all belong. Unrelated promotion and off-topic posts don't.

## What isn't allowed

We keep these as separate rules on purpose, because each one protects people in a different way.

**Scams and requests for money.** No asking members for money, gifts, or payment details. No fake offers, giveaways, "investment" schemes, fake charities, or links designed to trick people into paying or handing over information. This includes private groups and private messages that lead here.

**Paywalled or paid content.** No posts that require payment to access, or that exist mainly to sell something. Sharing a free resource that mentions where the original was published is fine.

**Copyright and "not your work."** Only share what you made, what you have permission to share, or what is clearly free to share. If you post something that isn't yours, mark it "Not my work" and credit the creator. If you believe your work was shared without permission, tell us (see "Contact") and we will follow a real notice-and-takedown process. [DMCA agent name, address and email — needed before launch.]

**Inappropriate content.** No sexually explicit material, graphic violence, or content that could harm children. No content that is illegal where we operate. Educational discussion of difficult topics is fine when it is handled with care and is clearly about education.

**Harmful files.** Every file is scanned for malware before anyone can download it. Files that fail are removed automatically. Don't upload files meant to damage devices or steal information.

**Harassment.** Don't threaten, bully, or demean anyone. Challenge ideas as strongly as you like; don't target a person.

**Off-topic and spam.** No repeated identical posts, link dumps, or advertising unrelated to education.

## Protecting children

Many of our members are parents and teachers writing about real children. Please don't post a child's full name, photo, school, address, or anything that could identify or locate them. When in doubt, leave it out.

## How we enforce this

- Anyone signed in can **report** a file, post, comment, or group, and choose a reason: scam or money request, off-topic, inappropriate content, paywalled or costs money, copyright or not their work, or something else.
- **A person always reviews a report before anything is done.** Nothing is removed or banned automatically because of a report.
- Depending on what the admins find, they may remove content, warn a member, or remove a member or group.
- If a scam or serious violation is **confirmed** after an admin has looked into it, we may post a public announcement so the community can protect itself. We only do this after confirmation, never on a bare accusation.

# Safety Policy

## Our promise

Always free. No ads. No scams. A small team of four volunteer admins reviews reports with real human judgment. We can't promise to catch everything — this page explains what we do, what we can't do, and how you can keep yourself and your family safe.

## Keeping children safe

- AOEhub accounts are for adults — parents, guardians, and teachers. [DECISION NEEDED: minimum age, and whether children may ever have their own accounts. See notes.]
- Never share a child's identifying details (see Content Policy).
- If a member asks a child for personal details, tries to contact a child privately, or asks to meet a child, **report it immediately** using "Something else."

## Scams and staying safe with other members

- **Never send money to another member**, and be cautious about anyone asking you to pay, "verify" your account, or move the conversation to another app.
- Be careful with links and downloads from people you don't know.
- **AOEhub is a place to share ideas and resources. Members are individuals, and we are not responsible for what one member does to another**, including scams or disputes between members. Please read our Terms of Service. [Link — final wording needs legal review.]
- If something feels wrong, report it. Reports go to the admins, who review them.

## Files and malware

Every uploaded file is checked with a third-party malware scanner before it can be downloaded, and files that fail the check are removed. This lowers the risk but can't reduce it to zero. Use your own device's security software, and report anything suspicious.

## Groups and privacy

- **Public groups** can be found by search and joined by anyone signed in.
- **Private groups** are invite-only and hidden from other members. "Private" means private *from other members* — **AOEhub admins can still see private groups so we can moderate them.** We only look when something is reported or needs review.
- Anything you share in a group can be seen by everyone in it. Only share what you're comfortable with them knowing.

## The AI assistant and translation

- The assistant can be wrong. Please double-check anything important, especially anything about a child's health, safety, or legal rights. It is not a substitute for a professional.
- The assistant is never part of discussions between members. When you ask another member a question, a person answers.
- Content can be shown to you in your chosen language using machine translation. Translations may contain mistakes; you can always view the original.

## If someone is in danger

AOEhub is **not** an emergency service and isn't monitored around the clock. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
